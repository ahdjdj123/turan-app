# TURAN — The Heart of the Silk Road

**An interactive journey through 8,000 years of Uzbekistan's history.**

TURAN is an interactive historical exploration platform dedicated to the rich heritage, civilizations, cultural identity and historical evolution of Uzbekistan.

From ancient civilizations and the legendary Silk Road to the formation of modern Uzbekistan, TURAN transforms historical learning into an engaging digital journey.

Rather than presenting history as a static collection of dates and facts, TURAN encourages users to discover, explore and connect with the events, people and places that shaped the region.

## ✨ Overview

TURAN brings together historical exploration and an interactive user experience, allowing users to navigate Uzbekistan's history through carefully organized discoveries.

**The experience includes:**

- **Historical Discoveries** — Explore 76 historical entries covering different periods of Uzbekistan's history.
- **Today's Discovery** — Encounter a featured historical discovery on the home screen.
- **Random Discovery** — Explore history through unexpected discoveries.
- **Interactive Journey** — Navigate historical content across an approximately 8,000-year historical timeline.
- **Personal Journal** — Keep track of your exploration and discoveries.
- **Leisure Settings** — Customize aspects of the experience.

## 🏛️ Historical Scope

TURAN explores the historical development of the region through different eras, including:

- Ancient civilizations and early settlements.
- The emergence of powerful historical states.
- The Silk Road and its cultural significance.
- Medieval scientific, intellectual and architectural achievements.
- The Timurid period and its legacy.
- The historical transformations of Central Asia.
- Modern and contemporary Uzbekistan.

## 🎯 Our Mission

To make Uzbekistan's history more accessible, engaging and memorable through interactive digital experiences.

TURAN aims to encourage historical curiosity, strengthen cultural awareness and introduce users to the extraordinary heritage of the region.

## 🌍 Why TURAN?

Uzbekistan occupies a remarkable position in world history.

Its cities, civilizations, scholars, architectural achievements and cultural traditions have contributed to the development of science, trade, art and intellectual life across continents.

TURAN seeks to bring these stories together in one accessible digital environment.

**History is not simply something to remember. It is something to discover.**
